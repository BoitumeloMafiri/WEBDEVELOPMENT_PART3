Our story

Maintenance Essentials started from a real, everyday frustration. After relocating to Johannesburg, our founder struggled to find a salon or nail technician she could trust, somewhere she could see real work, know the prices and the wait times, without spending hours watching TikTok videos. Her friends faced the same problem every month, so Maintenance Essentials was created.

Our mission

To make it effortless for young women in Johannesburg to discover, compare and book trusted beauty providers for their monthly maintenance, all in one reliable place.

Our vision

To become the go-to beauty-maintenance brand for young women in Johannesburg, and eventually across South Africa, and the platform every reputable salon and nail tech wants to be listed on.

How we keep it trustworthy

Every provider on Maintenance Essentials is vetted before being listed. Clients leave honest ratings and reviews, and providers who are consistently rated poorly are removed. That way, when you book through us, you can book with confidence.
